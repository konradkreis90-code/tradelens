How do I cancel my subscription?

In Peekline, open the paywall and tap Manage Subscription, or go to iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Peekline. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or period ends to avoid the next charge.

I paid but Pro isn't unlocked

Open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases, making sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I disconnect my brokerage?

In Peekline, go to Profile, tap View next to your brokerage, and choose Disconnect. The connection is read-only either way. Peekline can never place trades or move money.

How do I delete my data?

Email [CONTACT EMAIL] with your Support ID (shown at the bottom of the Profile tab in Peekline) and we'll delete what's stored for your install. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone.
