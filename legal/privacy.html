This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

2. Services we use

  • Anthropic — AI analysis of chart images and grading of closed trades. Anthropic processes the data to return a result; under its commercial terms it does not use this data to train its models.
  • SnapTrade (by Passiv) — the secure, read-only connection to your brokerage. SnapTrade's own privacy policy applies to the login and data it handles.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscriptions on top of Apple's App Store.
  • Finnhub — market prices. No personal data is sent.
  • TradingView — the interactive chart on the Chart tab is TradingView's widget and is subject to TradingView's policies.
  • Railway — hosts our server and database.

3. Read-only brokerage access

The brokerage connection is read-only. Peekline cannot place, change, or cancel orders or move money. You can disconnect at any time in the app (Profile → your brokerage → Disconnect), which removes our access through SnapTrade. You can also revoke access from your broker's own website.

4. How long we keep data

  • Chart images, analyses, positions, and trades: not stored on our server.
  • Daily analysis counts: deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Your app ID: kept while you use Peekline, so your connection and limits keep working. Ask us to delete it any time.
  • Brokerage connection: kept until you disconnect. Disconnecting deletes your SnapTrade user and the stored access key.
  • Server logs: our hosting keeps short-lived technical logs for troubleshooting. They record things like request counts and errors, not your holdings, prices, or account numbers.
