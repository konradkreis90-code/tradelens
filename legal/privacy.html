Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026 · Peekline is operated by Konrad Kreis ("we", "us").

Peekline is an iPhone app that reads stock chart images with AI and shows live prices, and can optionally connect to your brokerage account in read-only mode. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

2. Services we use

  • Anthropic — AI analysis of chart images, grading of closed trades, and portfolio explanations. Anthropic processes the data to return a result; under its commercial terms it does not use this data to train its models.
  • SnapTrade (by Passiv) — the secure, read-only connection to your brokerage. SnapTrade's own privacy policy applies to the login and data it handles.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscriptions on top of Apple's App Store.
  • Finnhub — market prices, sectors, earnings dates, and headlines. Only ticker symbols are sent, never your name, account, or amounts.
  • TradingView — the interactive chart on the Chart tab is TradingView's widget and is subject to TradingView's policies.
  • Railway — hosts our server and database.

3. Read-only brokerage access

The brokerage connection is read-only. Peekline cannot place, change, or cancel orders or move money. You can disconnect at any time in the app (Profile → your brokerage → Disconnect), which removes our access through SnapTrade. You can also revoke access from your broker's own website.

4. How long we keep data

  • Chart images, analyses, and brokerage data (balances, positions, orders, activity): not stored in our database; brokerage data is held in server memory for at most about 10 minutes.
  • Daily analysis counts: deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Waitlist email: kept until Peekline Pro launches and we've told you, or until you ask us to remove it.
  • Your app ID: kept while you use Peekline, so your connection and limits keep working. Ask us to delete it any time.
  • Brokerage connection: kept until you disconnect. Disconnecting deletes your SnapTrade user and the stored access key.
  • Server logs: our hosting keeps short-lived technical logs for troubleshooting. They record things like request counts and errors, not your holdings, prices, or account numbers.

5. Your choices

  • Disconnect your brokerage in the app at any time.
  • Cancel your subscription in the app (Manage Subscription) or in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Delete your data: in Peekline, go to Profile → Delete my data. This immediately deletes your brokerage connection (including at SnapTrade), your account record, usage counts, and waitlist email from our server, and your journal, history, and settings on that device. Purchase records kept by Apple and our subscription provider (RevenueCat) aren't affected; email peekline.support@gmail.com with your Support ID to ask about those.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU), you may have extra rights to access, correct, or delete your data. Contact us to use them.

6. Age

Peekline is for adults 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect and store.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and for important changes we'll tell you in the app.
